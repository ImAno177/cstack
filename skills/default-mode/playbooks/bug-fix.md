### Bug fix

Own reproduction, root cause, fix, and verification.

1. Reproduce the symptom on the matching surface and capture the failing evidence.
2. Form competing hypotheses and eliminate them with the smallest useful probe.
3. Fix the surviving mechanism at its boundary. Use `architect` when ownership or an interface changes.
4. Re-run the original repro and inspect the real artifact or side effect.
5. Report root cause, shipped fix, failing-then-passing evidence, skipped checks, and remaining risk.
